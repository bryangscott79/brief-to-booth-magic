Roles & Navigation

One shell, two sidebars. Agency members and admins live in the agency workspace; super admins get the platform sidebar — and can drop into any agency, read-only, through preview mode.

/admin is one route with two surfaces: agency admins (and super admins in preview) get Agency Settings — project types, clients & brand intelligence, agency KB, KB health, team. Super admins in normal mode get Platform Admin — all accounts, activation types, venues, image models, AI usage, invites.

How preview mode works
1

Enter

“Preview as Agency Admin” — from the platform sidebar’s dashed button or any account drill-in — flips the shell to the agency nav and lands on /projects.

Preview as Agency Admin
2

Browse

An amber banner pins above the nav for the whole session. Every agency page renders read-only, and Admin Settings shows the agency surface — never platform data.

Exit

3

Exit

The banner’s Exit link drops preview mode, returns to /admin, and restores the platform sidebar — crown identity, PLATFORM workspace label, five platform nav items.
